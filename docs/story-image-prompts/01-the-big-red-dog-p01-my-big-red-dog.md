# My Big Red Dog

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: The Big Red Dog
- Page: 1
- Source text: I have a dog. My dog is big. My dog is red.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a warm children's picture-book illustration of a cheerful child standing beside a big friendly red dog in a sunny yard. The dog must be clearly large and bright red, with a soft rounded body, short ears, gentle eyes, and a happy expression. The child should look proud and affectionate, standing close to the dog. Use simple background details like grass, flowers, and a bright sky. Keep the composition clear for young English learners.

## Continuity

Use the same big red dog design for all pages in this story. The child should remain visually consistent across the story.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, scary mood, realistic photo style, or cluttered details.
