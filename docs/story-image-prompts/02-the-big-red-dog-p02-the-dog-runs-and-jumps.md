# The Dog Runs and Jumps

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: The Big Red Dog
- Page: 2
- Source text: My dog can run. My dog can jump. My dog can play.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a bright children's picture-book illustration of the same big red dog running, jumping, and playing in a grassy yard. Show playful motion clearly: the dog can be mid-jump with legs lifted and tail wagging. The dog should look energetic, friendly, and safe. Add simple outdoor elements such as grass, a ball, flowers, and blue sky.

## Continuity

The dog must match Page 1: large, bright red, round friendly body, short ears, gentle expression.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, aggressive dog pose, or overly busy background.
