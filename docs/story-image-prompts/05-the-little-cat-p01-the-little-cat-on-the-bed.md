# The Little Cat on the Bed

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: The Little Cat
- Page: 1
- Source text: I see a little cat. The cat is on the bed.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a cozy children's picture-book illustration of a little gray cat sitting on top of a bed in a child's bedroom. The cat should be small, cute, round-faced, with big gentle eyes and soft subtle stripes. The bed should be clearly visible with pillows and a blanket. Use warm indoor colors and a calm safe mood.

## Continuity

Use the same little gray cat design for all pages in this story.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, messy room, scary shadows, or realistic photo style.
