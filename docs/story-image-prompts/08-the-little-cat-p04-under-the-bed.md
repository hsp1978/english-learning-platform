# Under the Bed

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: The Little Cat
- Page: 4
- Source text: Now the cat is under the bed. She is not on the bed.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a cozy children's picture-book illustration of the little gray cat peeking out from under the bed. Make the spatial relationship very clear: the bed is above, and the cat is underneath it, visible and safe. The cat should look curious, not scared. Use warm bedroom colors.

## Continuity

The little gray cat and bedroom should match previous pages.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, frightening under-bed scene, monsters, or dark horror lighting.
