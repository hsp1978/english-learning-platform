# The Red Barn

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: A Day at the Farm
- Page: 1
- Source text: We go to the farm. I can see a big barn. The barn is red.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a bright farm picture-book illustration of a child arriving at a sunny farm and seeing a big red barn. The red barn should be the main focus, large and easy to recognize. Add simple fences, green fields, and a blue sky. The mood should be welcoming and safe.

## Continuity

Use the same child design throughout the farm story.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, broken buildings, dark weather, or realistic photo style.
