# Morning Routine

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: My School Day
- Page: 1
- Source text: I wake up in the morning. I eat breakfast and brush my teeth.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a warm children's picture-book illustration of a child doing a morning routine before school. Show the child awake in the morning, with breakfast and a toothbrush visible as clear visual cues. The scene can combine bedroom and bathroom or kitchen elements in a simple friendly way.

## Continuity

Use the same child design throughout the school day story.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, messy bathroom details, unsafe objects, or realistic photo style.
