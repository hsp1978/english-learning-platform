# Teacher Reads a Story

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: My School Day
- Page: 2
- Source text: I go to school. My teacher is nice. She reads us a story.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a bright classroom illustration of a kind teacher reading a picture book to a small group of children. The main child sits with classmates and listens happily. The teacher should look warm and caring. The book should not show readable text.

## Continuity

Keep the same main child from Page 1.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, readable book pages, logos, watermarks, stern teacher expression, or cluttered classroom.
