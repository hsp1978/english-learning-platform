# The Big Blue Sea

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: Animals in the Sea
- Page: 1
- Source text: The sea is very big and blue. Many animals live in the sea.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a bright underwater children's picture-book illustration showing a wide blue sea with many friendly sea animals. The sea should feel large, open, and full of life. Include fish, gentle plants, bubbles, and soft sunlight from above.

## Continuity

Use a colorful underwater informational style across all sea animal pages.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, scary deep ocean, dangerous predators, or dark murky water.
