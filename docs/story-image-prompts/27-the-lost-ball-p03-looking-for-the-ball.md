# Looking for the Ball

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: The Lost Ball
- Page: 3
- Source text: Tom is sad. He looks under the tree. He looks behind the house. But the ball is not there.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a gentle emotional illustration of Tom searching for his missing red ball. Show Tom looking under a tree and near the side or back of a house. Tom should look sad but safe. The red ball should not be visible in this image.

## Continuity

Tom should match earlier pages. Keep the yard setting consistent.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, visible red ball, scary sadness, dangerous hiding places, or dark mood.
