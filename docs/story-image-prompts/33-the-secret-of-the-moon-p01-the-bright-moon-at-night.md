# The Bright Moon at Night

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: The Secret of the Moon
- Page: 1
- Source text: Look at the moon. It is big and bright in the sky. It shines at night.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a dreamy bedtime picture-book illustration of a big bright moon shining in a deep blue night sky. Add soft stars and gentle clouds. The moon may have a subtle friendly face, but keep it calm and magical.

## Continuity

Use the same moon and little star designs throughout this story.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, scary night sky, harsh contrast, or realistic astronomy diagram.
