# The Star Sings to the Moon

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: The Secret of the Moon
- Page: 3
- Source text: The star sings a song. The moon wakes up and smiles. They are friends.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a warm magical illustration of the little star singing near the moon as the moon wakes up and smiles. Show friendship and kindness between the star and moon. You may use soft glowing curves to suggest music, but do not include musical notes with text.

## Continuity

Use the same moon and star characters from previous pages.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, scary night, crowded stars, or overly realistic space scene.
