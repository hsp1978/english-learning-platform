# Rain for the Plants

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: Where Do Rainbows Come From?
- Page: 1
- Source text: Sometimes it rains. The sky is dark and wet. Plants like the rain.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a gentle weather picture-book illustration of rain falling from dark clouds onto green plants. The plants should look healthy and happy in the rain. The scene can be wet and cloudy, but it should not feel scary or stormy.

## Continuity

Use the same natural science picture-book style throughout the rainbow story.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, lightning, flood, fear, or heavy storm.
