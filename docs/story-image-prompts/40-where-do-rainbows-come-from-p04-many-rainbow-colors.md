# Many Rainbow Colors

## Nano Banana Brief

- Story: Where Do Rainbows Come From?
- Page: 4
- Source text: It has many colors. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple.
- Output: 16:9 horizontal image

## Prompt

Create a bright children's picture-book illustration of a large rainbow arching across the sky. The rainbow should clearly show bands of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. Add small green plants or hills below for scale. The scene should feel joyful, clean, and simple.

## Continuity

Keep the same natural setting and weather-story style from earlier rainbow pages.

## Do Not Include

No text, captions, speech bubbles, labels, logos, watermarks, missing rainbow colors, extra written color names, or photorealistic style.
